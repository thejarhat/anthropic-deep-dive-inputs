## Discrepancies to carry into the Copy (do not silently overwrite the filing)

- Q3 2026 revenue: S-1/A-2 **$17.0 billion**; spoken "nearly $17 billion"; slides 33–34 and 38 say **$17B** / **$17.0**; slide 43 table says **$16,956 million**. Keep the filed $17.0 billion and note the table's $16,956 million where that table is used.
- YoY growth: filing **1,127%**; spoken "up 12 times"; slide 33 "up >12x YoY"; slide 43 lists 1,127% for Q3. Consistent, filing more precise.
- Six-quarter 45x vs 10x: roadshow slide 38 / 35:06, not a six-quarter compute series in the S-1. The filing supports the revenue arithmetic ($376 million in Q1 2025 to $17.0 billion in Q3 2026). The 10x is a roadshow figure. The $900.9B committed spend is a separate table (slide 45) and is **not** in the 45x/10x in-period comparison.
- YTD adj. operating margin **12%** (filing and slide 33) vs Q3 adj. operating margin **15%** (slide 43 and 40). Both can be true.
- Gross margin: roadshow YTD **>85%**; the deck's 118 already derives Q3 gross margin **85.7%** and caveats that it counts only serving compute. Do not drop that caveat.
- Compute margin **43%** YTD: roadshow (slide 33), not found as a named line in the S-1/A-2.
- "Positive free cash flow" is spoken at 30:50. The filing reports **$6.6 billion of cash from operating activities** for the nine months ended 30 Sep 2026, and also says it expects significant net cash used in operating and investing activities going forward. Do not write spoken FCF as a filed fact.
- 11,900 customers: filing and slide 35 define them as **$100k+ TTM**. Spoken "enterprise customers." Slide 35 says **>7.5x** from ~1,500 at 31 Dec 2025; spoken "nearly 8x from the start of the year."
- Five advantages: slide 7 "Frontier and safety research"; spoken "frontier research and safety work."
- Opus 5.5 40% cut: filing "preserving our margins"; spoken "margin neutral"; slide 36 "maintain, or improve, margins."
- The 67% on slide 36 is a drop in Opus 4.5's cost, not the deck's 67% more tasks needed to hold sales after a 40% cut.
