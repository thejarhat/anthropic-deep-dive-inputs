How this was built:
- Candidates came from Post-Cutoff's archive of important X posts (postcutoff.com/posts.md, 591 X posts) and from recent-timeline mirrors (twiscan.com) of about 190 credible accounts: labs, benchmark orgs, data firms, analysts, investors and reporters.
- Web search turned up a few more, plus mirrors (nitter, twiscan, unrollnow).
- Every URL below was checked one by one against X's public syndication endpoint (cdn.syndication.twimg.com/tweet-result). That check returned the author, the date (UTC) and the media type for each post.
- Status: all of them are **verified**. One candidate came back deleted/withheld; I dropped it and used the same author's live repost.
- "chart, checked": I downloaded and looked at the first image. "image, not inspected": the post has a photo but I didn't open it. "image, not a chart": a logo, a headshot or a promo card.
- "already in Slack" marks posts shared in #lwm-anthropic-ipo. Everything else is new.

Line format: `[@handle: description](url) — date — has chart/visual? — why relevant — verified — new / already in Slack`
