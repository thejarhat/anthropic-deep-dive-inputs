Every claim on this page is sourced in the full deep dive; this page only shows how the pieces fit.

3. The shocks, sorted by evidence

Eight things could break the case. The framework sorts them by how much evidence exists that they are happening, because a shock already in the data deserves a different weight from one that's only imaginable. The bottom strip is the shared ground: conditions that hit every AI lab alike, so they move the sector's price without changing who wins.
